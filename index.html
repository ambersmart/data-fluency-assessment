A short check-up on how well your ministry uses data to make decisions — and whether what you measure matches what you value.

In addition to measuring the skills needed for data fluency, this assessment measures the value ministry team leaders place on effective data communication. Ideally your ministry is measuring what it values most — but that isn't always the case. This is designed to help you align your values, your measurement, and your actions.

Your answers are anonymous. We never ask for your name or email. Responses are grouped by campus and team so leaders can see patterns — not individuals.
